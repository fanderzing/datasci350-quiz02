	---
title: "Budget and Revenue"
---

Do bigger budgets lead to bigger box-office revenue? @fig-budget-revenue plots each film's production budget against its worldwide revenue, both in millions of US dollars.

```{python}
#| echo: true
#| label: fig-budget-revenue
#| fig-cap: "Production budget versus worldwide revenue for 104 films (millions of US dollars)."

import pandas as pd
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

films = pd.read_csv("films.csv")

plt.scatter(films["budget_musd"], films["revenue_musd"])
plt.xlabel("Budget (million USD)")
plt.ylabel("Revenue (million USD)")
plt.show()
```

---
title: "Runtime and Ratings"
---

Are longer films rated higher? @fig-runtime-rating plots each film's runtime in minutes against its public rating.

```{python}
#| echo: true
#| label: fig-runtime-rating
#| fig-cap: "Runtime (minutes) versus public rating for 104 films."

import pandas as pd
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

films = pd.read_csv("films.csv")

plt.scatter(films["runtime_min"], films["imdb_rating"])
plt.xlabel("Runtime (minutes)")
plt.ylabel("IMDb rating")
plt.show()
```

The table below shows the mean rating of the films in each decade.

```{python}
#| echo: true

films["decade"] = films["year"] // 10 * 10
films.groupby("decade")["imdb_rating"].mean().round(2)
```

---
title: "The Bond Films"
---

```{python}
#| echo: true

import pandas as pd
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

films = pd.read_csv("films.csv")
bond = films[films["franchise"] == "James Bond"]
```

The dataset includes the James Bond films released between `{python} int(bond["year"].min())` and `{python} int(bond["year"].max())`. @fig-bond shows how their worldwide revenue changed over time.

```{python}
#| echo: true
#| label: fig-bond
#| fig-cap: "Worldwide revenue of the James Bond films over time (millions of US dollars)."

plt.plot(bond["year"], bond["revenue_musd"], marker="o")
plt.xlabel("Year")
plt.ylabel("Revenue (million USD)")
plt.show()
```
